How artist accounts work

Upload beautifully

Upload your work in high resolution. PiXLMe is optimised to show on big-screen TVs — your work looks the way it's meant to.

What to upload

Ideal for TV

  • High-resolution photography (16:9 or wider)
  • Strong composition, rich colour, or dramatic black & white
  • Landscape, architecture, fine art, abstract
  • Personal lifestyle photography
  • Clear subjects with visual breathing room

Avoid

  • Distracting watermarks or text overlays
  • Low-resolution or heavily compressed files
  • Logos, brand marks, or inappropriate imagery

Helpful details

  • Clear, descriptive titles
  • Simple, specific tags
  • Collection grouping by theme or mood
  • Contextual notes help discovery

Payment & Tax Documentation

To process your earnings and issue accurate year-end tax statements (1099), PiXLMe is required by the IRS to collect a completed tax form from every artist. You will be prompted to submit the appropriate form when you set up your payment details.

US Artist

IRS Form W-9

Required for artists who are US citizens, US residents, or US-based entities. This form provides PiXLMe with your Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) so we can report your earnings and issue a 1099 at the end of each tax year.

  • Full legal name or business name
  • TIN (SSN or EIN)
  • Mailing address
Download W-9 Form (IRS) →
Non-US Artist

IRS Form W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E

Required for artists who are non-US individuals or foreign entities. These forms certify your foreign status and allow PiXLMe to apply the correct withholding tax rate based on your country's tax treaty with the US, if applicable.

  • W-8BEN — for individual artists (non-US persons)
  • W-8BEN-E — for foreign business entities

Your questions, answered

How do I start showcasing my artwork on PiXLMe?
Sign up as an artist on PiXLMe, upload your original artwork, categorize it, and start sharing with our global community. The upload process is straightforward — no technical setup needed.
What is PiXLMe's monetization model for artists?
PiXLMe operates on a revenue-sharing model. Artists receive 50% of revenue generated by their images, based on subscriber streaming engagement and downloads. The more your artwork is discovered and enjoyed, the greater your earnings potential.
What kind of artwork can I submit?
PiXLMe welcomes high-quality original photography, photographic art, paintings, and digital art. All work must be your original creation — no stock images or third-party owned content.
Can I track the performance of my artwork?
Yes. PiXLMe provides an artist dashboard with detailed analytics on views, engagement, and earnings — so you can understand your audience and grow your presence over time.
Is there a cost to join as an artist?
Artist accounts are free. Upload your work, set up your portfolio, and earn from subscriber engagement — no upfront cost, no listing fees.
How do I get my artwork featured in curated collections?
PiXLMe regularly showcases exceptional artists in our featured and curated sections. Produce quality content, use accurate tags, and our curation team will reach out for feature opportunities.
