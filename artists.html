Your questions, answered

How do I start showcasing my artwork on PiXL Me?
Sign up as an artist on PiXL Me, upload your original artwork, categorize it, and start sharing your creations with our global community instantly.
What is PiXL Me’s monetization model for artists?
PiXL Me operates on a revenue-sharing model, allowing artists to monetize based on image downloads, popularity, and subscriber engagement. The more your artwork is streamed and enjoyed, the greater your earnings potential. 50% of revenue allocated to artist pool.
What kind of artwork can I submit to PiXL Me?
PiXL Me welcomes a diverse range of original, high-quality, digital-ready images including photography, photographic art, paintings, and more. Please ensure all work submitted is your original creation.
Can I track the performance and popularity of my artwork?
Absolutely! PiXL Me offers an intuitive artist dashboard providing detailed analytics on views, downloads, subscriber engagement, and more—helping you understand your audience and grow your presence.
How do I get my artwork featured on the homepage or in special collections?
PiXL Me regularly showcases exceptional artists in our Featured Spotlight sections. Engage actively, produce quality content, and our curation team will reach out for feature opportunities and inclusion in special collections.
