Questions before you start?

How does the 14-day free trial work?
Sign up and get full access to PiXLMe for 14 days at no charge. No credit card required to start. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
What TVs are supported?
PiXLMe works with Samsung Smart TVs, Apple TV, Sony TVs, and Android TV devices. Check the app store listing for the full compatibility list.
How does the early adopter offer work?
Early adopters get their first year at $1.99/month. After the first year, pricing moves to the standard $3.99/month rate. This offer is available for a limited time during our soft launch.
Can I show my own photos on my TV?
Yes. PiXLMe lets you upload personal photos alongside curated collections, so your memories and professional art live side-by-side on your TV.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. After your trial, cancellations take effect at the end of your billing period.
