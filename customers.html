Your questions, answered

What is PiXL Me?
PiXL Me is an art-streaming platform designed to bring high-quality, display-ready images straight to your screens. Personalize your spaces by browsing and showcasing unique digital artwork effortlessly.
What types of images can I stream on PiXL Me?
PiXL Me provides an extensive range of carefully selected images, including photography, photographic art, fine art, and exclusive curated collections from talented creators worldwide.
Can I upload my own images to stream?
Yes! Subscribers can upload personal content to enhance their own art streaming experience. Simply use the upload feature in your PiXL Me account.
How do I customize the slideshow timing on my devices?
Easily customize how often images rotate by accessing your display settings. You can select from options such as Static (no rotation), Hourly, Daily, or custom intervals to perfectly suit your viewing preferences.
Which devices can I use to stream PiXL Me content?
PiXL Me seamlessly streams to multiple devices including Apple TV, LG and Samsung Smart TVs & Android TVs making your favorite images accessible anytime, anywhere.
