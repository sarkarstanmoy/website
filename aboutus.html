About Us

PiXL Me is designed to bring artists and art lovers together in an easy, efficient and affordable way. The basis of PiXL Me is a platform where the user has full control of the content they want to display. Curate from among the finest photographs and photographers available today. Customize what, how and where you want to display art – all within an intuitive and affordable way.


PiXL Me offers photographers a new and unique platform to reach a broader audience. The PiXL Me platform is based on a revenue share model, where we all share in the success of the platform!


Our goal is to be the best value proposition for those that enjoy photographic art. Enjoy in the home, office or any setting where people can be engaged.
